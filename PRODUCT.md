# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are owners and operations managers of Spanish SMEs (5–250 employees)
in clinics, law firms, accounting practices, logistics, industrial B2B and retail.
They run a business whose margin is eaten by manual, repetitive operational work
(invoices, quotes, inboxes, phone calls, scheduling), and they evaluate vendors
personally, often without a technical team. They read fast, compare two or three
vendors, and are skeptical of ROI claims they cannot verify.

Secondary audience: the occasional technical stakeholder (external IT partner or
in-house developer) who checks whether a vendor can actually ship and integrate
code, and who will disqualify a vendor whose site looks like a template.

## Product Purpose

IA4PYMES designs, builds, integrates and maintains custom software with AI for
SMEs: web applications, internal tools, process automation and private AI agents
connected to the client's ERP, CRM or knowledge base. Success means the client
runs software in production that measurably removes manual hours, and the
project's ROI was calculated before development started.

## Positioning

A software development company, not an agency: the internal team writes, integrates
and delivers the code, rather than reselling templates or hours of advice. The
differentiating commitment is that project ROI is calculated and measured before
development begins — if the numbers do not work, the project does not proceed —
and the 60-minute consulting session is credited in full against the development
budget if the client proceeds.

## Operating Context

Buyers arrive from search (mostly comparison and tool queries) and evaluate on
desktop. The buying motion starts with a paid 60-minute consulting session
(299 EUR + VAT) that produces an architecture and roadmap; there is a 15-minute
viability guarantee with a full refund if AI does not fit the business. Projects
are usually deployed on EU cloud infrastructure, with on-premise available where
required. Common integrations are Holded, Sage, Odoo, ERPs and CRMs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed services: custom software development; operational process automation
(invoice/document extraction, reconciliation, quote generation, inbox handling
with human-in-the-loop validation); conversational agents (web, WhatsApp, email)
and voice agents over the client's knowledge base; private LLM deployment on EU
cloud or on-premise.

Constraints: GDPR compliance and EU AI Act readiness are mandatory claims and must
stay true; client data is never used to train public models. Deployment is usually
cloud, so the site must not claim on-premise as the only option. Truthfulness
outranks persuasion: no unverifiable aggregate metrics (the earlier "+360% ROI
average / +1,000h saved per month" claims were removed for this reason).

## Brand Commitments

Name: IA4PYMES. Voice: direct, technical, warm, no hype, no slogans. Spanish
primary, English secondary. Brand accent colour is blue. The word "agencia"
(agency) is deliberately not used to describe the company.

## Evidence on Hand

Live product surfaces: a blog with technical articles (Spanish and English), and
sector/city landing pages under `/sectores`. Existing testimonials render on the
landing page and in the Review schema markup; their verifiability is the owner's
responsibility and is explicitly left unchanged and out of scope for design work.

Absences that future work must not fabricate: no verified customer logos, no
audited aggregate ROI figure, no case study with published before/after numbers,
no press mentions.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives: state what is built and what is guaranteed, never a
   number the owner cannot defend on a call.
2. Engineering credibility is the sales asset; the interface is the first sample
   of the team's craft.
3. Measure before building: ROI precedes code, and the site says so.
4. Sovereignty and compliance are product features, not footnotes.
5. Say what is true about deployment: EU cloud by default, on-premise on request.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is the required standard for text contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text);
the site's forms and secondary copy must satisfy it.
